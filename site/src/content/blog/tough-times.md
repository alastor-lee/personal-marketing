Welcome to the new site. It's built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and [Tailwinds](https://tailwindcss.com/).

## Why a website, now

It's become apparent that now more than ever having a space of my own is important.

It's fine to create profiles in other spaces, but this is my place to try things and talk about what I'm working on, why I'm working on it, and where I would like to go. I philosophize about the world on my own frequently, and in particular I think about how the work we do defines us. Meaning, the work we do and how we do it is fundamental to the worthwhileness of a large part of our time. I want to be someone that does great work for good reasons.
